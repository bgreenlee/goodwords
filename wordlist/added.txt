# Words the game will accept that the dictionary does not have.
#
# One per line, and optionally a definition:
#
#   word
#   word | part of speech | what it means
#
# Without a definition a word is simply accepted as valid. With one it can also
# be taught in the missed-words column and can name a round, so only give a
# definition to a word worth learning.
#
# The regular forms come along on their own, so one line per word is enough:
# "mosh | verb" brings moshes, moshed and moshing, and "selfie | noun" brings
# selfies without inventing selfied. The part of speech is what decides, so give
# it. An irregular word is the exception — list goose and geese separately.
